# Ontology of the Expanding Self

## Author
Kim Jaemin

## Overview
This repository presents the Ontology of the Expanding Self, a philosophical theory that understands the self as a dynamic and expandable structure formed through reflection, experience, and relationships.

The theory argues that self-expansion is the primary condition for ethical emergence, social transformation, and long-term human sustainability.

## Core Concepts

- The self is not fixed but expandable
- Metacognition enables self-expansion
- Self-inquiry is the method
- Desire must be structurally reflected upon
- The world is the field in which expansion occurs
- Ethics emerges from expanded recognition

## Structure of the Theory

Self-expansion  
→ Expansion of recognition  
→ Emergence of ethics  
→ Transformation of relationships  
→ Sustainable humanity

## Method

The process of self-expansion includes:

1. Analysis of personal history  
2. Deconstruction of internalized structures  
3. Emotional and behavioral pattern recognition  
4. Removal of imposed and unnecessary conditions  
5. Relational extension of the self  

## Central Proposition

The expansion of the self is the expansion of recognition and responsibility.

## One-Sentence Thesis

The self becomes larger through awareness, and in that expansion the boundary between self and other dissolves.
